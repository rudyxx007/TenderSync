# TenderSync Visual Direction

## Three design approaches

| Theme Name | Very Brief Intro | Probability |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Signal Room | A restrained decision-intelligence terminal with atmospheric maps, data traces, and decisive emerald signals. It communicates control in high-stakes bid work without mimicking a conventional dashboard. | 0.07 |
| Paper & Proof | An editorial, document-native environment that uses warm paper textures and annotated tender fragments to foreground evidence and rigor. | 0.04 |
| Field Ledger | A utilitarian operations ledger inspired by field notebooks, postal labels, and procurement artefacts, softened with refined contemporary typography. | 0.09 |

## Chosen approach: Signal Room

**Design Movement:** Quiet luxury enterprise software with Swiss information-design discipline and the atmospheric clarity of a modern command center.

**Core Principles:**
1. Make every important status, score, and decision visually immediate.
2. Use depth sparingly: dark mineral surfaces, hairline boundaries, and restrained light leaks rather than noisy glass panels.
3. Preserve an evidence-first hierarchy in which documents and decision signals receive more emphasis than decorative interface chrome.
4. Pair dense analytical views with generous negative space so complex information remains calm and legible.

**Color Philosophy:** The base is midnight graphite rather than black, establishing a grounded, confidential workspace. TenderSync emerald is reserved for forward momentum, positive opportunity, and primary actions. Warm amber marks uncertainty needing judgement; rose identifies hard gate failure; muted violet signals model/agent work. Light mode becomes pale limestone and ink, keeping the same disciplined signal vocabulary.

**Layout Paradigm:** The product is organized as a persistent left-hand operations rail plus an offset content field. Public marketing uses layered, full-bleed editorial bands with a right-anchored intelligence panel rather than a symmetrical centered hero. Analytical pages use a variable-width dossier canvas: document identity and key decision data form a top stratum, while evaluation details cascade in irregular but ordered blocks beneath.

**Signature Elements:**
1. The **Signal Arc**, a graduated emerald/amber/rose circular PWin gauge with fine radial ticks.
2. **Evidence rails**, thin vertical lines and small index labels which connect evaluation blocks to their data source context.
3. **Glowing aperture cards**, dark panels with a subtle inner edge highlight to frame high-value metrics and active pipeline stages.

**Interaction Philosophy:** Interactions acknowledge the urgency of bid work: tight 160–220ms feedback, quiet state changes, and intentional progressive disclosure. Primary decision actions have a soft signal bloom; routine navigation remains almost instantaneous.

**Animation:** Elements enter with 180–260ms opacity-and-translate motion in 45ms cascades. The PWin arc draws once upon result visibility, while the numeric score settles with a short spring. Pipeline states use a travelling signal dot along a thin route; motion pauses under reduced-motion preferences. No perpetual decorative animation.

**Typography System:** `Manrope` is the interface voice—compact, precise, and highly readable at small analytical sizes. `DM Mono` is used for timestamps, document references, percentages, and technical signals. Headline hierarchy favors Manrope 700/800 with tight tracking; body copy uses Manrope 400/500; labels use uppercased DM Mono with wide tracking.

**Brand Essence:** TenderSync is the decision-intelligence terminal for bid teams that need evidence-backed focus before committing scarce proposal capacity. **Personality:** exacting, composed, catalytic.

**Brand Voice:** Headlines are directive and evidence-aware; CTAs name the decision or artifact being created. Avoid broad claims and vague momentum language.

> Example headline: “Know which tender deserves your best team.”
>
> Example CTA: “Evaluate this RFP”

**Wordmark & Logo:** A compact aperture-shaped glyph made from two offset document corners and a rising signal arc, suggesting a tender entering a decision lens. The symbol stands alone in the application header and favicon; the wordmark pairs it with customized wide Manrope lettering.

**Signature Brand Color:** **Signal Emerald — `#10B981`**.

## Style Decisions

- The **Signal Arc** is a recurring decision primitive across public and product-facing surfaces. Its emerald, amber, rose, and violet variants explicitly distinguish opportunity, review, risk, and agent/model work.
- Marketing feature blocks are **evidence modules**, not generic feature cards: each carries a compact index, a source or confidence signal, and a status rail.
- Authentication remains intentionally focused, but adds asymmetric dossier traces and a signal-artifact field so account entry feels like crossing into an active decision workspace.
